The starting engagement

Studio & production
assessment.

A grounded answer to the decision ahead: does this milestone demonstrate progress, can this team execute the plan, and what needs to change?

What we review

The current build, milestone history, scope and roadmap, team capacity, dependencies, and production reporting. Interviews with studio leadership and discipline owners put that evidence in context.

What you receive

  • An executive decision brief with findings and supporting evidence.
  • Delivery risks, uncertainties, and their implications.
  • Practical options and prioritized actions with proposed owners.
  • A readout to discuss the findings and next review point.

We agree the decision, scope, access, timetable, and reporting recipient before work begins. The assessment makes clear where evidence is incomplete.

Executive decision brief

Upcoming vertical-slice milestone

Decision: What needs to be resolved before accepting the milestone?

Delivery confidence: at risk

Finding: The core loop is playable, but two critical dependencies remain unowned. Completion criteria differ between the roadmap and milestone checklist.

Evidence reviewed: Build review, dependency tracker, milestone checklist, and discipline-lead interviews.

Still uncertain: Performance on target hardware and the remaining integration effort.

Options & implications

Keep the milestone date: reduce scope and explicitly record what remains unproven.

Revise the milestone: preserve scope, estimate the integration work, and agree a new acceptance point.
