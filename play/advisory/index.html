01

Studio & production assessment

I play the build, talk with directors, leads, and production leaders, and take inventory of the plan, priorities, and available assets. I review the workflow and team organization.

You get: clear advice on what to change first, proposed owners, and a next review point. A concise summary supports the decisions.

02

Milestone advisory

Let’s look at the build, progress, and what’s getting in the way before your next decision.

You get: an experienced outside view. I come back to see what happened and help you adjust.

03

Hands-on production leadership

The work needs a leader with the authority and responsibility to move it forward. I lead a defined area of production with backing from studio leadership.

You get: clear direction. I own the decisions and follow-through within my remit, remotely or embedded.
